Spell-checking dictionaries shipped with Jacarium (Windows and Linux)
=====================================================================

The files in this directory are Hunspell dictionaries in Chromium's
compiled .bdic form, taken unchanged from the hunspell_dictionaries.zip
asset of the Electron release this app is built on
(https://github.com/electron/electron/releases). Chromium maintains them
in https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/deps/hunspell_dictionaries/
where each dictionary's full copyright notice and licence text is kept in
README_<language>.txt next to its source .dic / .aff files.

Jacarium does not change these dictionaries and does not download any: the
copies here are the only ones the app uses (electron/spellcheck.cjs).

  en-US-10-2.bdic   English (United States)
  en-GB-10-2.bdic   English (United Kingdom)
  en-AU-10-2.bdic   English (Australia)

  cs-CZ-3-0.bdic    Czech

The licence texts published with the dictionaries by the Electron release
are shipped beside this notice: LICENSE and COPYING (the aggregated texts:
Apache-2.0, LGPL-2.1, LGPL-3.0, MPL-1.1, GPL-3.0), COPYING.MIT,
COPYING.MPL, COPYING.GPL3, COPYING.LGPL, COPYING.LESSER and COPYING.Apache.
macOS builds of Jacarium contain none of these files: there the operating
system's own spell checker is used.
